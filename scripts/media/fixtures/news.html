Ferry service returns to the north pier after a decade

By Dana Whitfield · 6 min read

Three sailings a day resume on Monday, ending a stretch in which the north shore has been reachable only by road. The first boat leaves at 6:40 and takes nineteen minutes.

The old landing has been rebuilt with a longer ramp and a waiting room that opens onto the fish market. Tickets are sold on board for the first month.
